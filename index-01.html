The route from
first year to hire-ready.

Dependency-mapped skill trees. Tick a node when you can actually do it — progress follows your account.

Stage 01 · Fundamentals

Statics & Materials

Free-body diagrams, stress–strain, failure criteria. Nothing downstream works without this.

Parametric CAD

Sketch discipline, feature trees that survive edits, GD&T that a machinist can read.

Stage 03 · Analysis

FEA & CFD

Meshing, boundary conditions, and — the part everyone skips — validating the result.

Stage 04 · Application

Vehicle Dynamics

Load transfer, roll centres, tyre data, damper tuning. Where the maths meets the lap time.
